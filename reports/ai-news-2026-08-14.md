**Anthropic Files for September IPO at Near-Trillion Dollar Valuation**

Anthropic filed confidentially for an IPO on Monday, August 11, targeting a September or October listing that could value the company at $965 billion, [Bloomberg reported](https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-morgan-stanley-goldman-sachs-ipo-lead-banks). Morgan Stanley and Goldman Sachs are leading the offering, with JPMorgan Chase also on the deal. The company has been meeting investors in recent weeks, fielding questions about Chinese competition from models like [Kimi K3](https://cio.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/next-gen-technologies/kimi-k3-release-impact-on-anthropics-pre-ipo-valuation/133020436) and [Qwen 3.8](https://the-decoder.com/anthropics-planned-mega-ipo-faces-investor-skepticism-over-chinese-rivals-and-political-headwinds/), which have closed the capability gap with Western frontier models. Anthropic is pitching expansion into healthcare and biology applications while defending its $47 billion annualized revenue run rate, [driven primarily by Claude Code and enterprise API adoption](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/324065/20260812/anthropic-pitches-healthcare-ai-ipo-investors-fable-5-blocks-drug-research.htm).

**Federal Government Launches Secret Frontier AI Review Process**

A classified government review framework for frontier AI models has gone live under Executive Order 14409, signed June 2. The process, [led by an NSA-run group](https://aitoolbriefing.com/industry/frontier-ai-secret-government-review-2026/), gives federal agencies up to 30 days to review covered models before they reach the public. The capability thresholds and benchmarks that trigger review status are classified. White House staff met with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Microsoft, and Nvidia on August 4—three days after the deadline—to finalize implementation. Open-weight models are fully exempt from review regardless of capability level, creating a significant policy asymmetry as Chinese labs like Moonshot AI ship increasingly capable open models.

CEO Sundar Pichai highlighted particularly heavy adoption of voice interactions, live camera features, and multi-app workflows. The milestone puts Gemini on par with ChatGPT's reported user base. ## Major Releases & Product Updates

**xAI Launches Grok Bot Early Beta**

xAI released Grok Bot in early beta—a team of always-on autonomous agents that run on dedicated computers. The agents can sign into existing applications, learn workflows from user demonstrations, execute jobs in parallel, maintain memory of prior work, and operate continuously 24/7. Access is rolling out through higher-tier Grok and Cursor plans. [theneuron.ai](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-tuesday-august-11-2026/)

Meta released Muse Glimmer, a 30 billion parameter open-weight model under Apache 2.0 license, optimized for always-on local agents, coding, function calling, and model evaluation. The release targets laptop deployment for autonomous agent workloads. [aiweekly.co](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)

**Nvidia Opens Nemotron 3.5 Lightning**

Nvidia released Nemotron 3.5 Lightning, a 30B-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 3B active parameters that achieves gpt-oss-120b-level intelligence at a quarter of the parameters and up to 4x higher output speeds (~670 tokens/second on DeepInfra NVFP4 endpoints). The company also released an accompanying routing library. [aiweekly.co](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)

## Security & Research

**New Attack Decrypts Chain-of-Thought Reasoning**

Researchers discovered an attack against encrypted chain-of-thought blocks returned by proprietary AI APIs from Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google. While these companies conceal private reasoning generated by some models, they send encrypted versions back to clients for conversation continuity—creating a vulnerability that the new attack exploits. [theneuron.ai](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-tuesday-august-11-2026/)

**OpenAI's GPT-5.6-Cyber Discovers Chrome Zero-Days**

OpenAI's GPT-5.6-Cyber model discovered two previously unknown V8 vulnerabilities in Chrome that can be chained to corrupt memory and bypass the V8 heap sandbox. Google has patched them under CVE-2026-15903. GPT-5.6-Cyber responds to 95% of sensitive security queries (up from 57.3% for GPT-5.5-Cyber) and is OpenAI's first model to reach the 'High' cyber capability threshold under its Preparedness Framework. Hardware security keys become mandatory for all Daybreak accounts September 1. [aiweekly.co](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)

## Policy & Regulation

**EU AI Act Article 50 Now Enforced**

The EU AI Act's Article 50 transparency obligations became actively enforceable as of August 2, 2026. Anyone operating in Europe must now label AI-generated or AI-modified content and disclose when users are interacting with automated systems. Prohibition 9 (non-consensual explicit content) takes effect December 2026, with high-risk AI system obligations beginning December 2, 2027.
