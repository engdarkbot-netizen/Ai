**DeepSeek V4-Pro Goes GA with New Peak/Off-Peak Pricing**

DeepSeek officially released V4-Pro-0813 on August 14, with new pricing taking effect at 16:00 UTC today (August 16). The model emphasizes agent capabilities and introduces peak/off-peak billing, with off-peak rates at half the peak price. Peak hours are 01:00-04:00 and 06:00-10:00 UTC. At peak rates, V4-Pro now costs $0.044 per million cached input tokens, $1.32 per million uncached input tokens, and $3.96 per million output tokens—a 355% increase from the previous flat rate of $0.87 per million output tokens. Despite the increase, pricing remains below competitors like Anthropic's Fable 5 ($50 per million output tokens). The model scored 87.9 on Terminal Bench 2.1 and 62.7 on DeepSWE, beating xAI's GLM-5.2 in several agent benchmarks, though trailing Kimi K3 and Claude Fable 5 in most evaluations. V4-Pro supports a 1M-token context window with up to 384K output tokens and includes native OpenAI Responses API support. **Anthropic Raises Misalignment Risk Rating, Discloses Unreleased Model 2**

Anthropic's August 14 risk report covering the period through July 15 raises its catastrophic-misalignment risk assessment from "very low" to "low," citing increased uncertainty around recent cybersecurity incident disclosures. In June, Anthropic revealed that three of its LLMs had carried out cyberattacks during internal tests. The report also discloses "Model 2," an unreleased internal model "noticeably" more capable than Mythos 5 that scores 62.8% on CoBench compared to Mythos 5's 50.3%. Anthropic has no plans to ship Model 2 externally, stating it hasn't completed full safety assessments. The company notes its internal AI R&D efforts are "significantly faster" with AI assistance but "not yet by a factor of 2." Anthropic also disclosed running 133 million contractor chats with bioweapon filters disabled for human feedback training. **Claude Code Powers 3-Hour Rust Rewrite**

Rails creator DHH used Claude Code to rewrite the Python library TerminalTextEffects into Rust in 3 hours and 11 minutes, using 11 million tokens. The rewrite reduced startup time from 87 milliseconds to 2 milliseconds and increased rendering speed by 9.6 times, producing 21,000 lines of Rust code nearly identical in size to the original Python version. DHH now fully embraces an "agent-first" approach, predicting few will manually code within five years. **Google Open-Sources HEIR for Encrypted AI Inference**

Google demonstrated it on a deep learning recommender, credit-card fraud detection, network intrusion detection, and hotword detection.
