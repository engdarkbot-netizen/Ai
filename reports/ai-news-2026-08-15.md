## Top Stories

**OpenAI Classifies Upcoming Astra Model as Cyber-Critical**

OpenAI announced that internal evaluations of Astra, one of its upcoming models, indicate "significant advancements in agentic coding and cybersecurity." Based on these results and expert assessments, OpenAI classified Astra as having potentially Critical capabilities in Cyber under its Preparedness Framework. The company is pausing internal activities involving Astra that don't meet strengthened security requirements and implementing stricter controls including isolated testing environments, restricted network access, enhanced model weight protections, additional monitoring, and sandboxed execution. OpenAI also introduced GPT-5.6-Cyber for authorized cybersecurity work through expanded Daybreak Blue (for defenders) and Daybreak Red (for authorized vulnerability research) programs. ([thezvi.wordpress.com](https://thezvi.wordpress.com/2026/08/13/ai-181-astra-goes-cyber-critical/))

**xAI Completes Grok 4.7 Training with SpaceX Data Integration**

Elon Musk announced on August 12 that Grok 4.7 has finished initial training and is "significantly better" than the recently released 4.6. The model is now receiving supplemental training with a "massive amount" of SpaceX company data, with release expected in 3-4 weeks. Musk stated "the SpaceX training corpus is so awesome & unique that I would be shocked if any model is better at real-world engineering than 4.7." This follows the August 12 launch of Grok 4.6, which SpaceXAI says matches GPT-5.6 Sol on benchmarks. ([metatrader.com](https://www.metatrader.com/en/news/beincrypto/3777071-spacex-stock-adds-500-billion-for-an-ai-unit-that-lost-1))

**Grok 4.6 Released at Competitive Pricing**

SpaceXAI released Grok 4.6, pricing it at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens. The company says it matches GPT-5.6 Sol on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index with a score of 61, representing a five-point jump over Grok 4.5. The model is built for long-running agents, coding, and research tasks. Grok 4.6 is available through Grok Build, Cursor, and the SpaceXAI console. ([techmeme.com](https://www.techmeme.com/260812/p39))

**ChatGPT User Solves Major Math Problem**

Mathematicians report that a neurosurgery resident used ChatGPT, powered by GPT-5.6, to solve Crouzeix's conjecture, a major open problem in numerical linear algebra. ([techmeme.com](https://www.techmeme.com/260813/p9))
