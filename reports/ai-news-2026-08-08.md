## Security & Safety

**Zero-Click Vulnerability Compromises All Major Agentic Browsers**
Zenity Labs disclosed "PleaseFix"—a family of vulnerabilities exploiting "intent collision"—at Black Hat USA in Las Vegas. The technique allows attackers to hijack agentic browsers through malicious instructions embedded in ordinary web content, achieving account takeovers and remote code execution without user interaction. Zenity demonstrated successful exploitation of Perplexity Comet (patched February 2026), Claude in Chrome, Gemini, ChatGPT Atlas, and Copilot Edge. The root cause: agentic browsers removed Same-Origin Policy enforcement to enable cross-site automation, creating a security regression comparable to pre-2006 web browsers. "We can take over your social accounts, WhatsApp to all your friends on your behalf, buy things on your behalf in Amazon," said Michael Bargury, Zenity CTO. Some vendors characterized the behavior as intended functionality rather than a security flaw.
[darkreading.com](https://www.darkreading.com/endpoint-security/agentic-browsers-rewind-web-security-20-years), [labs.zenity.io](https://labs.zenity.io/p/perplexedbrowser-perplexity-s-agent-browser-can-leak-your-personal-pc-local-files)
