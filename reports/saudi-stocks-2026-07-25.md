## Company News

**Saudi Aramco** (2222) gained nearly 1% to SAR 26.84, benefiting from stable oil market conditions. The energy giant continues to be a key driver of TASI performance.

**ACWA Power** posted a 2.92% gain to SAR 194.00, reflecting sustained investor confidence in the Kingdom's renewable energy transformation under Vision 2030.

**EIC** (Etihad Etisalat - Mobily) surged 9.93% to SAR 14.83, marking one of the session's strongest performances and suggesting positive operational developments.

Banking stocks showed mixed performance, with **Al Rajhi Bank** slipping 0.47% to SAR 63.95 and **SNB** declining 0.85% to SAR 39.56.

**SABIC** and other industrials including Dar Al Arkan, Sipchem, Jarir, Al Abiya and SAIB declined between 1-3% as investors rotated into other sectors.
