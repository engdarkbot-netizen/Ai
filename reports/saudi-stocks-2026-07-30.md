**TASI: 10,507.02** (-1.60% from previous session)

## Market Outlook

**Technical Picture**: The TASI traded in a range of 10,497.44 to 10,647.35 on Tuesday, closing near the intraday low—a technically weak signal indicating bears maintained control throughout the session. The index is now testing the 10,500 support level ([businessupturn.com](https://businessupturn.com/asia/middle-east-asia/saudi-arabia/saudi-tadawul-slips-as-all-share-index-declines-1-60-at-close/)).

The sustained selling pressure appears driven by disappointing financial results and deteriorating market sentiment, with investors showing caution amid broader economic concerns.
