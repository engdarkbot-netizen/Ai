## Top Stories

**Anthropic Makes Claude Code Auto Mode Default This Week**
Starting August 14, Anthropic is making Auto Mode the default for Claude Code across Pro, Max, and Team subscriptions, eliminating manual approval prompts for most operations. [techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/09/anthropic-is-turning-claude-codes-auto-mode-on-by-default/) A classifier trained on dangerous command patterns will automatically block actions that are "irreversible, destructive, or aimed outside your environment." In testing with 1,053 paid testers, Auto Mode caught 89% of harmful actions versus just 13.6% for human review, though human performance dropped to about 5% after 50 prompts due to approval fatigue. [simonwillison.net](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/8/auto-mode/) Third-party evaluation by Trajectory Labs showed none of 720 indirect prompt injection attacks succeeded against Claude running Auto Mode. [mlq.ai](https://mlq.ai/news/anthropic-will-make-claude-codes-auto-mode-the-default-for-pro-max-and-team-users/) Enterprise and API deployments remain opt-in.

**SpaceX and Nvidia Partner on Orbital AI Infrastructure**
SpaceX announced on August 6 it is partnering with Nvidia to design the compute payload for Starmind AI1, the first satellite in a planned constellation built to run AI workloads directly in orbit. [teslarati.com](https://www.teslarati.com/spacex-and-nvidia-team-up-on-musks-orbital-ai-bet/) Each satellite will be equipped with Nvidia's Vera Rubin NVL72 rackscale system (codenamed Kyber), featuring Rubin GPUs and Vera CPUs. Elon Musk set a target of 2 gigawatts of compute capacity online by end of 2026, scaling to roughly 10 gigawatts by end of 2027. SpaceX has filed with the FCC for a constellation of up to one million satellites, citing constant solar power and absence of zoning restrictions as advantages over terrestrial data centers. ## Major Releases & Updates

**Anthropic's Auto Mode Safety Architecture**
Anthropic's Auto Mode uses a two-stage classifier with an input-layer probe for prompt-injection content and an output-layer transcript classifier that reviews user messages and tool calls. [mlq.ai](https://mlq.ai/news/anthropic-will-make-claude-codes-auto-mode-the-default-for-pro-max-and-team-users/) When blocked, Claude attempts a safer alternative or requests permission; after three consecutive blocks or 20 total blocks in one session, the system reverts to manual approvals. [aiweekly.co](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/anthropic-flips-claude-code-to-auto-mode-by-default-aug-14) The company will no longer charge Pro, Max and Team users for the classifier overhead tokens. Team administrators can set organization-wide defaults or disable Auto Mode entirely. Anthropic still recommends human review for high-stakes production infrastructure, noting "classifiers cannot eliminate risk."
