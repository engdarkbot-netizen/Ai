**Aramco Export Route Diversification:**
Aramco is studying expansion of its East-West Pipeline (currently at full 7M bpd capacity) and establishing new export routes following Iran's Strait of Hormuz closure and Houthi maritime blockade. The company is evaluating alternatives to reduce reliance on disrupted Gulf terminals.
