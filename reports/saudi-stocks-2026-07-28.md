## Company News

### SABIC Agri-Nutrients Reports Profit Decline

SABIC Agri-Nutrients Co. posted H1 2026 net profit of **SR1.6 billion**, down 21.4% year-over-year, according to interim results for the six months ended June 30. The company attributed the decline to:
- Lower sales volumes from supply chain disruptions
- Reduced contributions from associate companies and joint ventures
- Partially offset by higher average selling prices

Stock closed at SR121.50, up 0.57% despite the weak results.

*Source: [arabnews.com](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2652358/amp)*

### Scientific & Medical Equipment Wins SR199M Contract

Scientific & Medical Equipment House Co. secured a **SR199 million contract** to maintain and repair medical devices at hospitals and healthcare centers in the Asir region under the Ministry of Health's Asir Health Cluster. Financial impact expected to begin in Q4 2026. Shares closed 1.30% higher at SR27.54.

*Source: [arabnews.com](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2652358/amp)*

### Saudi Tadawul Group Q2 Results

Saudi Tadawul Group, the exchange operator, reported **Q2 net profit of SR92.1 million**, up 66% from Q1 2026's SR55.6 million. Operating revenues increased 10% quarter-on-quarter to SR323 million, driven by an 8% rise in non-trading-linked services.

However, stock closed nearly 3% lower at SR120.10 on Sunday, down 14% year-to-date. The Public Investment Fund owns 60% of the company.

For H1 2026, net profit dropped 32% year-over-year to SR148 million as operating revenue fell 5% annually, reflecting weaker market activity.

*Source: [agbi.com](https://www.agbi.com/markets/2026/07/tadawul-owner-reports-increased-profit-and-revenue/)*

## Macro Context

### Regional Tensions Continue to Weigh

The Saudi market remains under pressure following the escalation in regional conflicts. Earlier in July, TASI fell as much as 5% to 10,214 at open (lowest since March 2023) following U.S. and Israeli strikes on Iran and subsequent Iranian retaliation, including restrictions on Strait of Hormuz traffic—a critical route for one-fifth of global oil supply.

While the index has recovered somewhat to current levels around 10,766, investor sentiment remains cautious. The index is down **2.60% year-over-year** and has declined **3.55% over the past month**.

*Source: [tradingeconomics.com](https://tradingeconomics.com/saudi-arabia/stock-market)*

### Fed Week Uncertainty

Markets opened subdued ahead of the US Federal Reserve's upcoming policy decision, contributing to the cautious trading environment with turnover below recent averages.

*Source: [ae.marketscreener.com](https://ae.marketscreener.com/news/tadawul-shares-start-us-fed-week-subdued-saudi-tadawul-group-ticks-down-ce7f51dcdc80f526)*

### Oil Price Impact

Higher crude prices from regional disruptions could provide support to Saudi Arabia's economy as one of the world's leading oil exporters, potentially offsetting some of the negative sentiment from geopolitical tensions. However, the disruption to regional economic activity continues to weigh on investor confidence.

*Source: [tradingeconomics.com](https://tradingeconomics.com/saudi-arabia/stock-market)*
