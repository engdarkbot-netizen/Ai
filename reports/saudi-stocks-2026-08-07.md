## Macro Context

**Oil Prices:** Brent crude declined to approximately $79.26 per barrel (from $83.45 at week's start), representing a ~10% drop over three sessions. The decline stems from diplomatic progress on a potential 60-day agreement between the US, Iran, and Oman to reopen the Strait of Hormuz without transit fees. Iran confirmed reaching agreement with Oman regarding a navigation corridor, significantly reducing the geopolitical risk premium. [Source: bbntimes.com](https://www.bbntimes.com/financial/tasi-today-saudi-benchmark-holds-firm-at-10-891-57-as-banks-lead-rally-amid-geopolitical-crosscurrents)

**Market Decoupling:** The TASI's five consecutive positive sessions despite falling oil prices represents an unusual but notable decoupling, suggesting investors are increasingly pricing in domestic growth catalysts independent of crude prices. This reflects a maturing market where banking stocks with exposure to domestic credit growth, mortgage lending, and infrastructure spending are viewed as proxies for the non-oil economy.
