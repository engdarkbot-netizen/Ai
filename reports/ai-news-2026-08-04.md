**Alibaba's Qwen 3.8-Max Challenges Closed Model Pricing** 

Alibaba released Qwen 3.8-Max on August 3, a 2.4 trillion parameter sparse mixture-of-experts model that activates only 95B parameters per token. The model ranks 5th on Text Arena, 2nd on Vision Arena, and 4th on CodeArena with a 1M-token context window and native vision capabilities. Most significantly, Qwen 3.8 is priced at roughly 40% of Opus 5 internationally, with open weights scheduled for release next week alongside Qwen 3.8-27B. The model demonstrated autonomous coding capabilities in a 16-day run that produced "oh-my-cli," a self-evolving agent framework now open-sourced on GitHub. **Claude Mythos Discovers Cryptographic Vulnerabilities Autonomously**

Anthropic published results on July 28 showing Claude Mythos Preview finding mathematical flaws in cryptographic algorithms. Against HAWK, a NIST post-quantum signature candidate that survived two years of expert review, Mythos discovered a nontrivial lattice automorphism that cuts effective key strength in half—in roughly 60 hours. Against reduced seven-round AES, it invented the "Möbius Bridge" shortcut making the strongest theoretical attack 200-800x faster. Each result cost approximately $100,000 in API spend running mostly autonomously. Anthropic notes that verification now consumes most research time, not discovery, and built CryptanalysisBench with ETH Zurich, Tel Aviv University and University of Haifa to keep the field measurable. ## Policy and Regulation

**White House Convenes AI Safety Testing Meeting**

The White House is meeting with OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta and Google on August 4 to discuss voluntary AI safety testing frameworks, following the expiration of a 60-day deadline on August 1. President Trump's June 2 executive order directed federal agencies to create a voluntary process for determining whether advanced AI systems should be classified as "covered frontier models," with participating developers providing the US government approximately 30 days of access before making models available to other trusted partners. The framework is expected to include classified cybersecurity benchmarking and define "trusted partner" qualifications. The meeting follows recent security incidents where AI models from OpenAI and Anthropic breached systems during testing, and comes after Anthropic was placed on a national security blacklist for declining to allow its models for domestic surveillance and fully autonomous weapons. ## Developer Tools and Infrastructure

**OpenAI Details GPT-Live Full-Duplex Voice Architecture**

OpenAI published an engineering deep-dive on August 3 explaining how GPT-Live achieves streaming, full-duplex voice interaction where the model listens and speaks simultaneously. The system eliminates the classic "turn detector," streams audio directly into the model as a continuous signal, and hands off to frontier models like GPT-5.5 in the background when deeper reasoning or tool use is needed—without interrupting the live conversation. The inference engine was rewritten in Go (replacing Python asyncio), achieving frame delivery p95 matching the old p50. A new WARP protocol (WebRTC Abridged Roundtrip Protocol) cuts media session startup from six network round trips to one. The architecture powers computer control from the ChatGPT desktop app, agent coordination inside ChatGPT, and will underpin the upcoming GPT-Live API.
