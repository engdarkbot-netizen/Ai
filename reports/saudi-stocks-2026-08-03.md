# Saudi Stock Market Briefing – August 3, 2026

## Market Snapshot

**TASI:** 10,589.72 (+0.43% / +45.38 points) | **Volume:** SAR 4.6bn

The Tadawul closed Thursday's session in positive territory, with the index settling near its intraday high of 10,590.45, reflecting sustained buying interest through the close. The session saw 122 gainers versus 129 decliners across a trading range of 10,509.34 to 10,590.45.

**TL;DR:**
- Telecom (+1.46%) and Banks (+1.40%) led sectoral gains
- Insurance sector tumbled 4.03% on Tawuniya's 10% decline
- Foreign institutions were net sellers of SAR 570.9mn last week
- Several healthcare and consumer names reported strong Q2 earnings

Foreign institutional investors were **net sellers** of SAR 570.9 million on TASI during the week ended July 30, according to Tadawul's latest weekly ownership data. This marks continued foreign outflows as international investors reassess Gulf positions amid regional developments.
