## Company & Sector News

### Insurance & Services Strength

The **Commercial and Professional Services sector** rose 0.50%, while **Insurance** gained 0.46%, according to [businessmen-me.com](https://www.businessmen-me.com/en/1208/saudi-tasi-opens-slightly-higher-on-insurance-services-strength).

**Chubb Arabia Cooperative Insurance** reported interim results for H1 2026 ending June 30, showing insurance revenues increased 4.3% to SAR 199.37 million from SAR 191.06 million year-over-year. However, net profit after zakat fell 1.7% to SAR 3.91 million, down from SAR 3.97 million, per [arabnews.com](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2653502/amp). Q2 proved particularly challenging, with quarterly net profit plunging nearly 70% due to a 93% surge in insurance service expenses.

### Banking Sector Weakness

Contrary to earlier optimism, **banking stocks declined sharply**. Saudi National Bank fell 3.79% to SAR 38.10, while Al Rajhi Bank dropped 2.58% to SAR 62.20, according to [investing.com](https://www.investing.com/indices/tasi).

### Energy Sector

## Market Context

### Recent Volatility

The TASI fell as much as 5% to 10,214 at the beginning of August—its lowest level since March 2023—before recovering to around 10,700 as investors assessed implications of U.S. and Israeli strikes on Iran, according to [tradingeconomics.com](https://tradingeconomics.com/saudi-arabia/stock-market). The sharp decline came amid escalating tensions in the Middle East and Iranian threats to restrict traffic through the Strait of Hormuz, a critical route for approximately one-fifth of global oil supply.
