## Technical Outlook

TASI is testing critical support near 10,890 points after forming a bearish candlestick. The index has breached a short-term rising trendline on the RSI indicator. Key levels to watch:
- **Support:** 10,890 (immediate), then 10,800 and 10,730 (61.8% Fibonacci level)
- **Resistance:** 10,930 (immediate), then 10,970 and 11,000

Analysts caution that a decisive break below 10,890 could trigger further profit-taking toward the 10,730 level. [aljaziracapital.com.sa](https://www.aljaziracapital.com.sa/media/gtslpfsk/daily-mon-29-06-2026-en.pdf)
