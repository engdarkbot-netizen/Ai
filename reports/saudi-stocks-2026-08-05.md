## Regulatory & Market Structure

**Foreign investor access expanded:** Saudi Arabia's removal of the Qualified Foreign Investor (QFI) framework in February 2026 continues to reshape market dynamics. The reform opened the Main Market to all foreign investor categories, aiming to diversify the investor base and improve liquidity. [waya.media](https://waya.media/saudi-equity-market-slows-in-h1-2026-despite-higher-market-value/)

**H1 2026 flow data:** For the week ended 30 July, foreign institutions were net sellers of SAR 570.9M on TASI, accounting for 40.27% of buy transactions and 43.05% of sell transactions. Saudi retail investors were net buyers (SAR 103.7M), while Saudi corporates added SAR 444.3M in net purchases. [argaam.com](https://www.argaam.com/en/article/articledetail/id/1925210?amp=)

## Macro Context

**H1 2026 performance:** TASI ended the first half at 10,799.92 points, down 3.26% year-over-year, while the Nomu Parallel Market Index declined 15.69% to 23,051.10 points. Despite the index weakness, market capitalization increased 3.4% to USD 2.52 trillion (SAR 9.44T). [waya.media](https://waya.media/saudi-equity-market-slows-in-h1-2026-despite-higher-market-value/)

**Trading activity softened:** Total traded value in H1 fell 10.4% YoY to USD 164.42 billion (SAR 616.6B), with executed trades down 11.8% to 52.69 million. However, average daily volume rose 9.5% to 133.3 million shares. [waya.media](https://waya.media/saudi-equity-market-slows-in-h1-2026-despite-higher-market-value/)
