# Saudi Stock Market Briefing – 8 August 2026

## Market Snapshot

**TASI:** 10,891.57 | **Change:** +0.04% (+4 points)

The Tadawul All Share Index extended its winning streak to five consecutive sessions on Thursday (6 August 2026), demonstrating resilience despite a ~10% drop in oil prices this week. The index closed marginally higher at 10,891.57, supported by strong banking sector performance that offset energy sector weakness.

**Key Takeaways:**
- Banking stocks led the rally, with the sector up 1.40%, while energy stocks declined despite Saudi Aramco's marginal gain
- Market decoupled from falling oil prices (Brent crude down to ~$79.26/barrel) on diplomatic optimism regarding Strait of Hormuz
- Trading value reached SAR 4.6 billion with mixed breadth: 122 gainers vs 129 decliners
- Index trading in mid-range of 52-week span (10,193.83 - 11,781.68)

## Macro Context

**Oil Price Decline:** Brent crude has fallen approximately 10% over three trading sessions this week, from $83.45 to around $79.26 per barrel. The decline stems from reports that the **US, Iran, and Oman** are close to a temporary 60-day agreement to reopen the Strait of Hormuz without transit fees, easing geopolitical tensions. [bbntimes.com](https://www.bbntimes.com/financial/tasi-today-saudi-benchmark-holds-firm-at-10-891-57-as-banks-lead-rally-amid-geopolitical-crosscurrents)

**Market Resilience:** Despite the oil price decline—significant given oil's role as Saudi Arabia's primary economic driver—the TASI has maintained its upward trajectory. This reflects a notable leadership rotation from energy to banking stocks and suggests market confidence in domestic economic diversification efforts. [bbntimes.com](https://www.bbntimes.com/financial/tasi-today-saudi-benchmark-holds-firm-at-10-891-57-as-banks-lead-rally-amid-geopolitical-crosscurrents)
