## Top Stories

**Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Max Claims Benchmark Dominance Over GPT-5.6 Sol and Claude Fable 5**

Chinese tech giant Alibaba launched Qwen3.8-Max, a massive 2.4 trillion parameter mixture-of-experts model that reportedly outperforms OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol and Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 across 7 coding/agentic/general capability benchmarks and 36 multimodal/vision tests. The model is available now via QwenCloud, with model weights being open-sourced on Hugging Face and ModelScope next week, intensifying US-China AI competition. [neowin.com](https://www.neowin.net/news/alibaba-releases-qwen38-max-challenging-gpt-56-sol-and-claude-fable-5-on-ai-benchmarks/)

**EU Begins Enforcing AI Act Transparency Requirements**

The European Union began enforcing key provisions of its AI Act on August 2, requiring companies to clearly disclose when users are interacting with AI systems and to label deepfake content with both visible markers and machine-readable watermarks. General-purpose AI providers must now document their models, publish training data summaries, and address systemic risks including cyberattacks and loss of control, while enforcement is divided among the European Commission's AI Office, national regulators, and the European Data Protection Supervisor. [theaiinsider.tech](https://theaiinsider.tech/2026/08/03/eu-begins-enforcing-ai-act-as-transparency-rules-take-effect/)

**OpenAI-Anthropic Rivalry Intensifies Over Math Problem Claims**

OpenAI published a blog post claiming its unreleased Astra model solved or made substantial progress on 10 long-standing open problems in mathematics and theoretical computer science, spanning coding theory, quantum complexity, and lattice cryptography. Within 24 hours, Anthropic engineer Levent Alpoge responded that Claude Fable—already publicly available—autonomously solved 5 of those same problems without internet access, including arithmetic circuit complexity and quantum parallel repetition challenges. [indiatoday.in](https://www.indiatoday.in/technology/news/story/openai-says-its-unreleased-astra-model-solved-10-hard-math-problems-anthropic-claims-fable-cracked-5-2962067-2026-08-03)

## AI Research & Technical Breakthroughs

**DeepSeek's Lightweight Model Beats Flagship Through Post-Training Alone**

DeepSeek's V4 Flash model achieved a paradigm-shifting result by outperforming the company's own V4 Pro Preview flagship model—which has roughly 8x more parameters—through post-training optimization alone. V4 Flash scored 82.7 on the Terminal-Bench 2.1 coding benchmark compared to Pro Preview's 72.4, while costing just $0.28 per million output tokens versus Claude Opus 4.8's $25, directly challenging conventional AI scaling laws. [Seoul Economic Daily](https://en.sedaily.com/finance/2026/08/03/ai-war-breaks-weight-class-deepseek-lightweight-model-beats)

**China's Moonshot AI Narrows Gap with US Models**

Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 model scored 57 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, coming within 2-3 points of top-tier US models, with industry assessments suggesting the US-China AI technology gap has shrunk to just 2-3 months. This represents a significant acceleration in Chinese AI capabilities alongside Alibaba's and DeepSeek's recent advances. [Seoul Economic Daily](https://en.sedaily.com/finance/2026/08/03/ai-war-breaks-weight-class-deepseek-lightweight-model-beats)

## Business & Industry News

**Industrial AI Transformation Accelerates in Manufacturing**

Korean startup INEEJI, founded by former KAIST professor Choi Jae-sik, has accumulated 11.67 million tons of process data from continuous manufacturing industries (steel, oil refining, cement) and is deploying AI autonomous operation solutions that act as "AI factory managers." The company was designated the first AI firm holding national strategic technology status in Korea and has signed contracts with Japanese steel and cement companies, while the Korean government plans to expand AI factory projects to 200 by 2027 and 500 by 2030. [Seoul Economic Daily](https://en.sedaily.com/finance/2026/08/03/ai-war-breaks-weight-class-deepseek-lightweight-model-beats)

## Policy & Regulation

**EU AI Act High-Risk Provisions Delayed Until 2027-2028**

While transparency requirements took effect August 2, the EU postponed requirements for high-risk AI systems until December 2, 2027, with rules covering high-risk AI embedded in regulated products delayed until August 2, 2028. Additional prohibitions on systems generating nonconsensual sexually explicit material or child sexual abuse content are scheduled for December 2, 2026. [theaiinsider.tech](https://theaiinsider.tech/2026/08/03/eu-begins-enforcing-ai-act-as-transparency-rules-take-effect/)
