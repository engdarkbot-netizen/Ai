## Market Context

**Recent Volatility**: The previous week saw TASI drop 2% to 10,590 points, marking the largest weekly loss in three months. Declines were driven by mixed corporate earnings, liquidity cost fluctuations, and ex-dividend trading, though weekly trading value rose 10.3% to SAR 20.55 billion, indicating active position reallocation.

**Liquidity Costs**: 3-month SIBOR declined to 4.87% following the Federal Reserve's decision to hold rates steady, supporting a banking sector rebound in the last session despite most bank stocks continuing their weekly decline.

**Earnings Impact**: Market displayed clear selectivity - SABIC fell 6.2% after reporting quarterly losses, while companies with positive results like Solutions and Americana gained. Thursday's rebound helped recover some losses but doesn't indicate a trend change, as broad revaluation pressure continues.
